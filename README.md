# Value-at-Risk or Expected Shortfall for Insurer Solvency Capital? — replication package

Zenodo DOI: pending (release v1.0.0)

Code, aligned data and results to reproduce every table and figure of

> Elbehairy, M. (2026). *Value-at-Risk or Expected Shortfall for Insurer Solvency Capital? Evidence from Mature, Egyptian and Saudi Portfolios.* Manuscript, Department of Statistics and Insurance, Faculty of Commerce, Suez Canal University.

## Abstract

Solvency II sets insurer capital at a one-year value-at-risk (VaR) of 99.5 percent, a convention Egypt and Saudi Arabia are now deciding whether to adopt. The paper measures how much the choice between VaR and expected shortfall (ES) matters once the loss distribution is estimated from observed data, and whether a Solvency II-style market charge covers the tail. Six U.S., EEA, Egyptian and Saudi insurer portfolios are built from 5,388 daily observations on eighteen series (2010–2026), and their one-year 99.5 percent loss is estimated by a Gaussian model, historical simulation, a stationary block bootstrap and a Student-t copula under two drift conventions, with daily backtests of static and volatility-tracking models. Under normality ES exceeds VaR by 1.5 to 2.9 percentage points in the mature portfolios; on the bootstrap distributions the gap is 2.2 to 3.9 points and the loss itself is larger. The Gaussian model fails every daily backtest; a filtered historical simulation passes the coverage test in all four portfolios. For Egypt the result turns on the drift: a Gaussian VaR crediting the 12.2 percent nominal carry is −0.2 percent, a zero-drift VaR is 12.0 percent and the real-terms bootstrap loss is 14.0 percent, against a market charge of 11.7 percent.

| `01_core_estimates.py` | Builds the aligned daily return panel from the raw price/rate files in `data/`, forms the six portfolios, computes Gaussian / historical / block-bootstrap / t-copula VaR and ES at 95, 99 and 99.5 %, the simplified standard-formula comparator, stress-window diversification and the Egyptian real-return table (Tables 1–3, 5, 8). | `sol_results.json`, `ports.json`, `data/daily_returns.csv`, `data/prices_aligned.csv`, `data/weights.csv` |

## Data

All inputs are observed market prices or official rates, included in `data/` as downloaded:

* `global_daily.csv` — S&P 500, Euro Stoxx 50, VNQ, LQD, IEF, BIL (Yahoo Finance).
* `euro_saudi_daily.csv` — iShares Core EUR Govt Bond, Core EUR Corp Bond, European Property Yield, eb.rexx Money Market; SP Funds Dow Jones Global Sukuk ETF (Xetra / Borsa Italiana / NYSE Arca via Yahoo Finance).
* `egx30_tv_daily.csv`, `egx_tmgh_tv_daily.csv`, `egx_realestate_tv.csv` — EGX 30 and the four listed developers (Egyptian Exchange feed).
* `tasi_tv_daily.csv`, `saudi_reits_daily.csv` — TASI and the twelve Tadawul-listed REITs (Saudi Exchange feed).
* `fx_daily.csv` — EGP per USD (Yahoo Finance).
* `egypt_policy_rate.csv` — Central Bank of Egypt overnight deposit rate by decision date.
* `saibor3m_monthly.csv`, `kapsarc_interest-rates-and-sama-average-bills.csv` — 3-month SAIBOR (SAMA via KAPSARC).
* `gem_cpi_m_EGY.csv`, `gem_cpi_m_SAU.csv` — monthly CPI (World Bank Global Economic Monitor).
* `ecb_dfr.csv` — ECB deposit facility rate (not used in the final portfolios; kept for completeness).

Derived files (`daily_returns.csv`, `prices_aligned.csv`, `weights.csv`) are regenerated by step 01. Market data are redistributed for replication only; rights remain with the respective providers.

## Method summary

* Six portfolios (weights in `ports.json`): conservative / balanced / growth U.S.; EEA-representative (EIOPA mid-2024 shares); Egypt nominal and real-terms (stylised deposit-heavy allocation); Saudi (Insurance Authority 2025 asset mix). Sample 2010-01-05 to 2026-09-24; Saudi from 2020-01-02.
* Estimators: Gaussian closed forms; overlapping 250-day historical simulation; stationary block bootstrap (mean block 60 days, 20,000 paths, seed 2026); Student-t copula on monthly returns (100,000 paths). Drift conventions: with-drift (Table 3) and zero-drift / demeaned (Table 4).
* Simplified standard-formula market charge: Delegated Regulation 2015/35 shocks (39/49 % equity, 25 % property, 168 bp parallel rate shock on fund duration, 1.4 %·duration spread, 25 % currency), prescribed correlations, symmetric adjustment 0, no concentration or look-through.
* Backtests: rolling 500-day window; 99 % VaR and 97.5 % ES; Kupiec, Christoffersen, Acerbi–Székely Z₂; EWMA λ = 0.94 for the dynamic models.
